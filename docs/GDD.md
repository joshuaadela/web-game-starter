# Game Design Document

> Living document, owned by the `game-designer` agent. Update it whenever a design decision is made.
> Status: **template**. Replace the prompts below with your game's design.

## 1. Pitch
_One or two sentences: what is the game and why is it fun?_

## 2. Pillars
_Three design pillars every feature must support._

## 3. Core loop
_Second-to-second, minute-to-minute, session-to-session._

## 4. Match structure
_Players per room, match length, win/lose conditions, respawn rules._

## 5. Systems
_List each gameplay system (movement, combat, items, scoring, progression, ...) with a short description and link to its spec._

## 6. Art direction
_Visual style, palette, readability rules on small screens._

## 7. Monetisation & live-ops
_Cosmetics? Ads? Premium? Decide early, implement late._
